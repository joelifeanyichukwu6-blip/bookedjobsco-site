How the pilot works

We do the setup. You do the jobs.

Most local service companies don't lose jobs because of bad work. They lose them because someone else answered first. The pilot fixes the first five minutes.

  1. Week 1: You tell us your towns and prices. We build the ad, the form, the instant text, the call script and your booking calendar.
  2. Weeks 2 to 4: Ads run only in your towns. Every lead gets a text in under 60 seconds and a call within 5 minutes.
  3. Every Friday: A plain-English report. Leads, how fast each was reached, what got booked, what it cost.
  4. Day 30: You judge it on booked jobs. Keep going or walk away. You pay the ad platform directly.

Questions owners ask

Are the numbers in the demo real?

No. Names and numbers in the demo are examples to show how it works. Your real results depend on your area, your prices and how fast your team answers. That's why we start with 30 days.

No. You get texts and calls like you do now, plus a calendar link and a weekly report by email.

Who pays for the ads?

You set the budget and pay Facebook directly. We never mark up your ad spend.

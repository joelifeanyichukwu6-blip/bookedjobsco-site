Straight answers

Common questions.

What do I actually get?

Local Facebook ads that only show in your towns, so homeowners who've never heard of you can find you and reach out. Their requests come straight to you, the jobs go on your calendar, and you get a Friday report in plain English.

Does this work for my trade?

It works best for local service businesses where people search for help when something breaks, like septic, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, garage doors, tree work and water damage. We talk it through on the call and I'll be honest if your trade isn't a good fit.

What does the free test cost me?

No setup fee, and you don't pay me anything for the test. We run it for 1 to 2 weeks and try to get you 2 or 3 extra jobs from new customers. We go over the details on the call.

What happens after the test?

If you like what you see, we do a 30-day pilot. We agree the price on the call before anything starts, so there are no surprises. If you don't like it, we stop. No long contract.

I already get my work from word of mouth and referrals.

That's great, it means you do good work. This doesn't replace it. It adds new customers on top, people who would never have heard your name otherwise.

I've tried Facebook ads before and they didn't work.

Most ads fail because they show to the wrong people or the wrong area. I keep the ads only in your towns and only for the jobs you want, so the people who see them can actually hire you.

Will the leads be shared with other companies?

No. The ads run in your company's name and the leads come straight to you. They're yours.

How fast will I see jobs?

The free test usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. Ads can start bringing leads in the first few days once they're approved.

How much of my time does this take?

A 15-minute call to start, then you do the jobs. I handle the ads and getting the requests to you.

If we stop working together, who keeps everything?

You do. The ad account is yours, the leads are yours and the customers are yours.

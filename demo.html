How the pilot works

We do the setup. You do the jobs.

Most septic companies get their work from word of mouth and referrals. That's great, but it only reaches people who already know you. The pilot puts your name in front of homeowners in your towns who have never heard of you.

  1. Free test first: 1 to 2 weeks where we try to get you 2 or 3 extra jobs. No setup fee, and you don't pay us anything for the test.
  2. Week 1: You tell us your towns and prices. We build the ads, the form and your booking calendar.
  3. Weeks 2 to 4: Ads run only in your towns, so homeowners who never heard of you can find you. Their requests come straight to you and go on your calendar.
  4. Every Friday: A plain-English report. New people who reached out, booked jobs and revenue.
  5. Day 30: You judge it on booked jobs. Keep going or walk away. No long contract.

Questions owners ask

Are the numbers in the demo real?

No. Names and numbers in the demo are examples to show how it works. Your real results depend on your area and your prices. That's why we start with a free test.

Do I need to learn any software?

No. You get texts and calls like you do now, plus a calendar link and a weekly report by email.

I already get work from word of mouth. Why would I need this?

You don't replace it. Referrals only reach people who already know you. This adds new customers on top, people who would never have heard your name otherwise.

What does the free test cost?

No setup fee, and you don't pay us anything for the test. We go over the details on a quick call.

What if it doesn't work?

No long contract. After the test, and again after 30 days, you look at the booked jobs and decide.
